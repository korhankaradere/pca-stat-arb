The signal is real

Before costs every strategy earns a Sharpe ratio above 1: stocks that drift away from their factor-implied path do tend to come back.

Costs decide the outcome

The original strategy earns about 2.6 bps per unit traded and pays 5. Drag the cost below 2.5 bps and it turns profitable.

Timing is the hidden cost

Trading one day after the signal removes 20–45% of the gross profit. Part of the apparent edge comes from the bid-ask bounce at the close.
